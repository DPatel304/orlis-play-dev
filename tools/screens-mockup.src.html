Orlis Play

Two squares, one hopping onto the other: a blue square (#3da9ff) lower left and a violet one (#8b5cf6) upper right, tilted 10 degrees. No face. "Play" is the blue to violet gradient. Cubo stays the game character.

1. Home: one page, no tabs

The focused game's painted world fills the screen. Big title, one line, players and time, one big Play. Then the rows: "Your games", "New to try" (what Discover was) and "Make a game" (Create). Scroll down for more. Grown-up things are behind the profile face. The game title keeps a lettering of its own (here a stand-in: the cover's painted titles are on the tiles).

2. Home in hand mode

Same layout. The camera is on; a child points, a wave chooses, and holding still never starts anything. The cursor, ring and face chip are in the colour of whoever is pointing. The profile face grows into the one big corner button.

3. Pause and results

The same content, in the clean look: a flat white card with a soft shadow, Outfit, near-black buttons, and the hand ring in the pointing child's colour.

4. Sign-in and Who's playing?

At TV size in Big Picture: big fields and big faces, driven by the remote or by hand. The painted game art stays behind, softly blurred.

5. Loading

The one wait pattern everywhere: the two squares do a little hop, with the game's name card, while the game starts underneath.

Open questions

  1. Hero title lettering. The cover tiles carry each game's own painted title, but the big hero title here is a stand-in (outlined Outfit bold). Should the hero reuse each cover's title style, drawn large?
  2. Home corner in hand mode. The logo is top left and the profile face top right (big in hand mode). There is no separate Home button on the home page. On other screens (Create, a request) do we show the big Home pill in the left corner, replacing the logo?
  3. Sign-in typing. On the TV the fields are for show: typing comes from the phone or a keyboard. Is a "type on your phone" code step wanted here instead?
  4. Pause over the game. The pause card hides part of the game. Should it be smaller and sit at the left, as here, or full-width at the bottom?
