# 0009: Watches as controllers
Status: Shelved
Date: 2026-11-20
## Context
Nov 15-16 chose the Bangle.js 2 watch and a Starter Kit with watches. The vision now centres on iPads and phones.
## Options
1. Shelve watches; keep the controller protocol general so a watch could join later. (Recommended.)
2. Keep watches as a second controller type.
## Notes
A camera already sees the whole body. A watch adds wrist motion, taps, a buzz and identity, which a phone can mostly provide,
and it does not suit children under 3.

## Update (2026-11-20)
The owner asked whether watches make tracking four people easier. Mostly not: the hard parts are identity (who is who after people cross or leave), occlusion and the camera model's speed, and a watch does not reduce the camera's work. A watch could help identity by matching wrist motion to a skeleton (sensor fusion), but everyone must wear one and it is complex. Identity can be handled by the line-up step, colour-coded avatars, and pad seats, which are bound to a person. Keep shelved; revisit if real four-person testing shows identity problems.

## Update (2026-11-20)
Shelved by the owner: not in the Phase 1 or Phase 2 plans; revisit only if a game needs it.
