# 0012: Two ways games are made
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-20
## Context
Create mode in the app is for kids and families to make regular games with the kit. The owner wants premium games too, and may
hire a video game developer to help "vibe code" them.
## Decision (recommended)
- **Create** (in the app): description-based, kit-only, free, private to the family until approved.
- **Premium games:** built by vetted developers (the owner, a hired developer, later approved studios) using the dev kit and
  Claude Code, with the same kit and sandbox but more power (for example 3D worlds and pad programs). Every premium game is reviewed
  and approved by the owner before sale or rotation.
- Premium games are sold once, or rotate free for a week for parents to try (see 0010).
## Consequences
Needs a clear dev kit, a review checklist, a submission path, and a revenue share for outside developers.
