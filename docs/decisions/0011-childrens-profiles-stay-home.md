# 0011: Children's profiles stay in the house
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-20
## Context
0007 put kid profiles on the owner's server. The owner is unsure about storing children's data and asked what happens if none
is stored.
## Decision (recommended)
- **Grown-up accounts only on the server:** an email, a sign-in, what the family owns (the library, purchases).
- **Child profiles stay in the house:** a first name or nickname, an avatar and progress live on the Orlis Box, and are shared with
  the family's own iPads over the home network. Camera pictures never leave the device.
- **Backup is the parent's choice:** an exported file, or a copy the parent keeps; nothing about a child is on the owner's server.
## Consequences
Greatly reduces the owner's responsibility under children's privacy law, because the owner does not collect it. A lost box means
lost profiles unless backed up. Cross-box features (leaderboards between families) are not possible without sharing data, so
they are out of scope.
## Replaces
The "kid profiles on the owner's server" line of 0007.
