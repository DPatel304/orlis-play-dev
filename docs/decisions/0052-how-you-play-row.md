# 0052: Home shows players as an icon and a number, and a "how you play" row; the tablet icon is a tablet in a controller grip
Status: Accepted (2026-11-23)
Date: 2026-11-23

## Context
The owner looked at how Nintendo shows play modes and player counts (an icon with a short label), and asked to bring back the number
and to show things like tablet support.

## Decision
- **Players:** one people icon and the number ("1–2", "2", "1–4"), not a row of figures.
- **A "how you play" row** under the description: small painted icon + one or two words, quiet white, no boxes, only what is true for
  the game, at most three items: players; **Move** (played with the camera); **Tablet** (a tablet is needed) or **Tablet optional**;
  **Phones** (phones as controllers); **Sit back** (a story, nobody has to move). Built from what each game already declares.
- **The tablet icon is a tablet held in a game-controller grip** (sticks, buttons and a D-pad on the two handles), not a bare tablet.
- **A small tablet mark in the corner of a game's tile** when the game needs a tablet, so a family knows before picking it.

## Replaces
From 0047/0048: players drawn as one to four figures with no number.
