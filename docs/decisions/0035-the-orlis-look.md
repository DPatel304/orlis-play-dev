# 0035: The Orlis look: cozy, playful, handmade paper, for every kind of game
Status: Accepted (the cube-head avatars to confirm after the owner sees the sketches)
Date: 2026-11-20

## Context
Orlis is a platform of many games, like Roblox: the visuals must be versatile, fixed by the kit, so players have freedom over the
experiences, not the art. The owner likes the current painted look and Paper Mario's feel (as inspiration, not a template), and asked
for one look that is simple, iconic, and works in 2D and in a fully 3D paper world.

## Decision (the owner's answers)
- **The feel:** cozy, playful, handmade.
- **Who it is for:** simple enough for toddlers to read, with enough attitude that older kids don't outgrow it.
- **The material is paper only.** Variety comes from world palettes and shapes, not other materials; gameplay is where games differ
  (as in Roblox and Minecraft).
- **Any genre is welcome**, because the visuals are controlled by the kit (the safety floor in 0013 still applies).
- **3D is a fully 3D paper world** (free camera), not only dioramas.
- **Every player is a cube-headed paper figure** (direction chosen; first sketches in `docs/cube-avatars.html`): one body shape and one
  face style for everyone; faces, hair, hats, skin tone, head shape, glasses and clothes are customised. Cubo moves onto the same body.
- **The signature (proposed):** the cube head, the white paper edge, and a soft shadow on the layer behind every cutout.

## Next
A one-page style rulebook (layers and shadows, what gets an ink outline, flat colour with one shade, matte highlights, world palettes,
paper motion) and a style board of very different games drawn with it.

## Consequences
- The avatar maker and the avatar art change to cube heads; existing profiles need converting.
- Cubo's look changes (same head, the shared body).
