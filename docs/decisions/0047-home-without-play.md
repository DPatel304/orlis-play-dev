# 0047: Home without a Play button; players drawn as figures; the description on the left
Status: Accepted (2026-11-23)
Date: 2026-11-23

## Context
With 0046 built, the owner found the hero's Play button redundant: picking the lit tile already starts the game (remote OK, a click,
a wave). The "1-2" players text read as numbers rather than people, and the description ran towards the picture.

## Decision
- **No Play button on home.** The tile is what you pick. The only button left in the hero is "Open Discover" when the library is empty.
  Remote Up from the first row goes nowhere (there is no Play to reach).
- **Players are figures:** one person for a 1-player game, two overlapping (one in front of the other) for 2 or more. No numbers.
- **The description wraps on the left**, in the plain dark area, never over the picture; larger text (about TV reading size).
- No time, no "A story." tail.

## Replaces
From 0045: "one big Play" in the home hero. From 0046: players as an icon with a number.
