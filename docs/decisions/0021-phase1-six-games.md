# 0021: The games for Phase 1
Status: Accepted (two games to confirm; the tiers are proposed)
Date: 2026-11-20
## Context
Phase 1 should have a set of games, each perfected, each showing a different style of play. The owner first named six, then added three more
that are already built and liked.
## Decision
| # | Style | Game | Status today |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arcade, reach and pop | Pop Party | Built; tuning |
| 2 | Board game | Save the Harvest *(to confirm)* | First cut built |
| 3 | Narrative, passive | Storytime (Cubion's Long Way Home) | Built |
| 4 | One pad plus the TV | Peekaboo Garden, one-pad form (0015) | New; needs the pad page (M2) and the stage |
| 5 | Runner | A simple temple run, reusing Last Known Position's road | New |
| 6 | Fitness | Animal Gym *(to confirm: or a new fitness game)* | Built |
| 7 | Creative sandbox | Sky Painter *(added)* | Built; plain cream background, needs the house look |
| 8 | Quiet and still | Sleepy Bear *(added)* | Built; close to the house look |
| 9 | Flying | Pterry Flight *(added)* | Built; a flat green look that needs restyling |

**Two tiers (proposed):** the **core six** (1-6) meet the full bar below. The **three added games** (7-9) meet a lighter bar: no known
bugs, the house look and the standard HUD, and one family playtest, without a deep tuning pass. This protects the schedule.

**"Perfected" (the full bar) means:** played by the owner's under-3 children without help; tuned from family playtests; no known bugs and a clean
first minute; in the house look; the standard HUD; a measured smoothness line on the owner's laptop.
## Consequences
Restyling work, biggest first: Pterry Flight, Sky Painter, Save the Harvest; Sleepy Bear and Animal Gym are close. Peekaboo Garden is now part of the plan, not a stretch goal (this updates 0015). It depends on M2 (the browser pad page) landing by
mid-January and on the 3D stage. The runner and Peekaboo Garden are the schedule risk; the other four exist already.
