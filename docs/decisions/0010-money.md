# 0010: How Orlis makes money
Status: Open (deferred to after Phase 1)
Date: 2026-11-20
## Context
Hardware sales are the main income. Games are free. The owner wants other income, possibly an optional small subscription, and
likes the League of Legends "free for a week, then pay to keep" idea, without making game-making harder.
## Options
- An optional family plan: a hosted family server, cloud backup, more making slots and credits (AI costs real money).
- Premium kit packs (a theme of assets and stage pieces) free to try for a week, then bought or rented. Playing and making stay
  free and easy; extra tools cost.
- A rotation of premium games free for a week, aimed at parents, with no countdown pressure on children.
- A revenue share on premium games.
## Conflict
Nov 16 decided "no subscriptions". An optional subscription would reverse that.
## Update (2026-11-20)
- **An in-game currency is deferred:** skipped for now (the owner's call). The earn-only idea below stays as a note for later.
- The owner likes: more making slots, premium kit packs, a rotation of premium games, and is open to an optional premium
  subscription ("Orlis Plus").
- **An in-game currency** is wanted. Recommended shape: an *earn-only* soft currency (earned by playing and making, spent on
  cosmetics and unlocks) kept separate from real money; real money buys things for the family through a parent (plans, packs,
  premium games). Selling a currency for real money to children is where regulators and the "never" list say no.
  Name ideas: Cubits, Pops, Sparks, Moves, Pips.
- **Bitcoin for hardware:** possible through a payment processor that handles it and can settle in dollars; it is a business
  side addition, not an architecture question. Check tax treatment and refunds with an accountant.
- **Premium games** are made by a vetted developer, not by Create (see 0012).

## Never (unchanged)
Ads to kids, selling data, loot boxes, purchases by kids.

## Update (2026-11-20)
Deferred by the owner: decide after Phase 1 with real family feedback. Phase 1 is free; the ideas above stay noted.
