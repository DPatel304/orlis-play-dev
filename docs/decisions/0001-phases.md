# 0001: The phases
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-20
## Context
The product is large. It needs a first stage that is small enough to finish and learn from.
## Decision
- **Phase 1 (beta):** the owner runs Orlis Play on a Windows laptop and plays with family (a wife and two children under 3).
  A brother-in-law and the owner's father also get it; they may make games and share them to the marketplace. The pad app
  (Android) is completed in this phase so the owner can see how it works. Purpose: test gameplay and making games.
- **Phase 2 (the dev kit):** a dev kit is sold. By then more games exist, the asset kit is more complete, and Create mode is
  more complete.
- The ready-made Orlis Box comes after, eventually (see 0003). All hardware work is outsourced.
- **Target date for the Phase 1 beta on the family's devices: March 27, 2027.**
- A video game developer may be hired to help "vibe code" premium games for the marketplace (see 0012).
## Consequences
- The pad app is Android only in Phase 1; Apple support comes in Phase 2 (0016, 0018).
- Phase 1 needs no Apple developer account or Mac; those come with the iPad and iPhone app in Phase 2 (0018).
- Children under 3 mostly play through the camera, not through iPads.
