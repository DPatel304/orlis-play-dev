# 0065: Weekly challenges have no "minutes" challenge, and games made in Create never count
Status: Accepted (2026-11-25)
Date: 2026-11-25

## Context
The weekly challenges (0051) were three a week: rounds of one game, some different games, and every other week "Move together for 30
minutes" (else rounds of a second game). Rounds of games made in Create are not sent to the server at all. The owner (2026-11-25): made games
should not count toward weekly challenges, and the minutes challenge goes.

## Decision
- Every week's three challenges are: rounds of one game, some different games, and rounds of a second game. No minutes challenge.
- Rounds of games made in Create never count toward any challenge (as built: they are not reported to the server).

## Consequences
- The challenges are about which games a family plays, never about time spent.
