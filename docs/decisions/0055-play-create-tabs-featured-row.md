# 0055: Two tabs, Play and Create; Discover is reached from a Featured row on Play
Status: Accepted (2026-11-23)
Date: 2026-11-23

## Context
Without tabs (0045's one-page home), getting new games and making your own were easy to miss. Mockups of top tabs, a side rail and a
row of round buttons were compared on the real home screen.

## Decision
- **Top tabs beside the logo: Play and Create** (the current one a white pill, the other quiet). Remote: Up from the first row
  reaches the tabs; Left/Right moves between them. Profiles that may not create (kids, 0028) see no tabs bar, just the logo.
- **Play's first row is "Featured":** this week's three featured games (from Discover, not yet on this PC), then a fourth tile,
  **Discover**, that opens the Discover page. Then "Your games". The hero (picture on the right, description, players and PlayPad
  row) works the same for featured games; picking one gets it (with its progress on the tile) then plays.
- **Discover is its own page - the app store** - with the same clean look, a Home/Back way out, and the games by category.
- **Create** is the existing Create page under the Create tab.

## Replaces
From 0045: "one page, no tabs" and the "New to try" / "Make a game" rows on home. 0047's "Remote Up from the first row goes nowhere".
