# 0037: Pop Party stays painted for Phase 1; the toon look is tested fairly before it is final
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-20

## Context
0036 chose a toon-shaded 3D look and asked for Pop Party to be redrawn in it before the beta. The first toon mockup of the HUD and home
(`docs/ui-toon.html`) disappointed the owner: the balloons no longer stood out and the painted warmth was lost. Part of that was the
sketch (scenery as bright as the balloons, no haze, the same outline on everything, no painted detail), part is real: simple 3D shapes
do not get the painted look's life for free.

## Decision
- **Pop Party stays the painted 2D game for Phase 1** (the redraw in 0036 and plan package PP2 are dropped).
- **Peekaboo Garden stays the 3D trial**, aiming to look like the same world as Pop Party (0033): its palette, painted sky and feel.
- **0036 is under review:** a fair side-by-side (the real painted Pop Party against an art-directed toon version with calm, hazy scenery,
  bright outlined balloons with highlights and a painted sky) decides whether the toon look is final, or whether 2D games stay painted
  and 3D games borrow the painted palette and skies.
- The date stays May 28, 2027 for now; dropping the redraw gives it back some room.

## Replaces
The Pop Party redraw in 0036's owner's answers; plan package PP2.
