# 0060: Create is "Cubo's Create": made with your body, guided by Cubo
Status: Accepted (2026-11-24)
Date: 2026-11-24

## Context
0059 kept Create as a picture builder driven by the remote. The owner (2026-11-24) wants Create to be easy from the TV and made with
motion: only "Cubo's Create", an animated Cubo guiding each step. Storyboard: `docs/cubo-create-mockup.html`.

## Decision
- **Create is only Cubo's Create.** Each step is a small motion game with a few big things to touch, never a grid of small tiles.
  Cubo says each step once, in a few words, then stays quiet while you choose; he walks, points and reacts to picks.
- **The steps:**
  1. **Start:** Cubo waves you in ("Raise both arms to start"); the family's games (3 slots) sit along the bottom.
  2. **Where:** step left or right and the world behind you changes; arms up to choose.
  3. **The game:** four big choices, Collect, Dodge, Keep up, Stack, each showing Cubo doing it; touch one. **One kind per game.** The kind
     decides how you play, so there is **no "Your move" step**.
  4. **What:** pop one of five group bubbles (Food, Animals, Sky, Toys, Nature); that group's things ride a slowly turning wheel, about
     8 at a time; reach into the glowing spot at the top to grab one; swipe to spin. Pick 1 to 5 (a tray shows them); arms up when done.
  5. **Rules (or skip):** two big sliders pushed by hand, much wider than before: speed in seven steps from a snail to a rocket (about
     0.3x to 3x), and how many from one at a time to a storm (about 25 on screen).
  6. **Name:** jump to spin a wheel of goofy names (3 to 5 words); arms up to keep one.
  7. **Play it** straight away; then Play again, Change it (back into Cubo's Create), or Home.
- **Only the first player** (the first person the camera admits) builds; others watch.
- **The remote works too,** as a quiet backup on the same screens (for someone on the sofa or a camera that isn't working): arrows move
  between the big choices, OK picks.
- Voice is not part of this (still paused, 0057); 0049 (Home has no motion) is unchanged.

## Replaces
From 0059: the remote picture-builder screens, one job per thing (now one kind per game), and the move-card step. 0059's things-with-jobs
data, goofy names and start-screen banner carry over into Cubo's Create.
