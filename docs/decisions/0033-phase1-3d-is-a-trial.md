# 0033: Phase 1's 3D game is a trial: consistent and smooth, gameplay may be unfinished
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-20

## Context
0026 put two perfected games in Phase 1. The owner clarified (0029): most Orlis games will be 2D; Phase 1 trials 3D to prove the
ecosystem can make 3D games that look consistent with the 2D ones. Peekaboo Garden's gameplay may stay a work in progress.

## Decision
- **Pop Party is the one perfected game.** Peekaboo Garden ships in the beta as a clearly-labelled trial ("try our first 3D game").
- **Peekaboo's release bar:** (1) it looks like the same world as Pop Party (signed off with the look check); (2) it runs well: 60 fps on
  the TV with tracking on (owner's laptop, then the Box hardware), 60 fps on the minimum phone (0030); (3) it can be played start to
  finish without bugs. Fun and tuning are welcome but not required.
- The work saved goes into the look, performance, and the pads, which every later game uses.

## Consequences
- The plan's gate item for Peekaboo changes from "perfected" to the three points above; the family playtest at M3 becomes a "does it
  read and run" check, with gameplay notes kept for later.

## Replaces
The "two perfected games" part of 0026 (the game choice and the TV-plus-phone design stand).
