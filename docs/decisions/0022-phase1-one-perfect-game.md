# 0022: Phase 1 ships one perfected game and a finished platform; the other games come after
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-20
## Context
0021 planned nine games for Phase 1. The owner changed course: perfect Pop Party (Balloon Pop) and that is all; feel good about the
graphics and the kit; finish the other games later. Once one game and "all the other stuff" are done, the owner releases to the
brother-in-law and father, and after that focuses on games.
## Decision
- **Release gate for the closed beta (target March 27, 2027):** Pop Party perfected (the full bar in 0021), and the platform done:
  the look and the kit settled, pads working (browser page, seats, reconnect and pause), Create usable by the two testers with the
  bigger kit and the approval step, the closed-beta install and invite flow, and kid profiles on the Box (0011).
- **The other games** (Save the Harvest, Storytime, Animal Gym, Sky Painter, Sleepy Bear, Pterry Flight, and the new Peekaboo Garden
  and runner) stay playable as they are and are finished after the beta is released. The owner wants to feel good about their graphics
  and the kit now, so some are restyled to the house look as proofs (which ones: to confirm).
- **Pads are proven on Pop Party:** pad players join Pop Party as extra hands (up to 8 seats, up to 4 tracked), which exercises the
  seats model, the pad page, reconnect and pause on a game that is already perfect.
## Consequences
Removes the runner and Peekaboo Garden from the Phase 1 schedule (they become the first games after release). The schedule risk moves
to the platform work, mainly the pad protocol (M2). The 0021 list stays as the roadmap for the games after release.
## Replaces
The Phase 1 scope in 0021 (superseded in scope; its list and "perfected" bar still stand) and the Phase 1 timing in 0015.
