# 0030: Phase 1 pads are the testers' own phones, with a minimum
Status: Accepted (minimum: iPhone 15 or equivalent)
Date: 2026-11-20

## Context
0026 named "an Android tablet" for Peekaboo Garden's hider. The owner then said the testers will use their own phones (mostly decent,
newer models, mostly iPhones), with agreed minimum requirements. The owner's own test phone is an iPhone 11, on which an earlier 3D
spike ran at 30 fps.

## Decision
- **Any phone or tablet with a current browser** joins as a pad (the browser page, 0019), iPhone and Android alike.
- **Minimum for the 3D view (Peekaboo's hider):** iPhone 11 or newer on a current iOS (Safari), or an Android phone from 2021 or newer
  with a mid-to-high-end chip on current Chrome. Target 30 fps on the minimum, 60 on newer phones.
- **Minimum as a controller (Pop Party's extra hands):** any phone from the last six years with a current browser.
- **Test devices:** the owner's iPhone 11 is the floor; one Android phone (bought for testing, 0016) is the second; the testers' phones
  are checked with a one-minute "phone check" page before the beta (it reports the model class, 3D support and a frame rate).

## Consequences
- No tablet purchase is required; phones in portrait or landscape both have to work for the hider view.
- The native Android app stays a stretch (0019).

## Replaces
The "Android tablet" wording in 0026 (the game choice in 0026 stands).

## Owner's answer (2026-11-20)
**The minimum is an iPhone 15, or an equivalent Android** (a 2023 flagship chip or newer: Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 class, Tensor G3, Dimensity
9200 class), for every role, including the 3D view. Target 60 fps on the minimum. The owner's iPhone 11 is below the minimum, so it is no
longer the test floor; an Android tablet at about the minimum's level is bought for testing (recommendation in the chat of this date).
