# 0014: No online play
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-20
## Context
Gaming has become too isolated and too online; Orlis is about people moving and socialising in the same room.
## Decision
Two houses playing together is out of scope. The Store, sign-in and updates are the only uses of the internet.
## Consequences
Messages between devices are kept independent of how they travel, so this can change later. Adding online play later would be
mostly a safety, legal and moderation job (relays, accounts, chat, children's privacy), not only code.
