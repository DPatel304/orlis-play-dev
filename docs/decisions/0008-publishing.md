# 0008: Publishing and guardrails
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-20
## Context
The owner makes most games at first; family may publish; later every grown-up may submit.
## Decision
- **Phase 1:** the owner publishes. The brother-in-law and the father may also make and publish games.
- **Phase 2:** every grown-up account can submit a game for approval; the owner approves.
- **Guardrails:** creators use only what is in the kit and cannot make assets; visuals are guarded (nothing visually
  questionable); the word filter and prompt guard stay; the kit keeps games looking consistent.
- Content spans all ages. Each game carries an age band; the box has parental controls (to design).
