# 0053: Two phases - a small PC beta now, a box later
Status: Accepted (2026-11-23)
Date: 2026-11-23

## Decision
- **Phase 1: a beta on Windows PCs with family and friends** - two testing families. They install Orlis Play and join the owner's server
  over the internet with today's sign-in and approval. No new setup flow is built for them; the owner helps them in person or on the
  phone, with a one-page guide.
- **Phase 2: Orlis sold as a box** with the software preinstalled: setup is plug in, Wi-Fi, an account made on the phone, play. That
  needs an Orlis online service for accounts, Discover and requests (a change to 0044), children's-privacy rules, and the box's own
  operating system. Planned when Phase 1 is done.
- The setup spec and mockup (`docs/specs/setup.md`, `docs/setup-mockup.html`) are kept for Phase 2 and not built now.
