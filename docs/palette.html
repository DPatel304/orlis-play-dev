The Orlis master palette

One set of colours for both looks, 2D and 2.5D. It is taken from what Pop Party and the 2.5D garden already use, tidied into groups so every new game and kit piece picks from the same list. Approved as decision 0040; games ask for these colours by name.

Touchables: the brightest colours

Only things a player touches, catches or picks (balloons, stars, buttons in a game). Nothing in the scenery may be this bright. These are Pop Party's eight balloon colours, with Mint and Lagoon softened so all eight feel like one set and Lagoon stands out against water.

The rules that come with it
  • Touchables are the brightest objects on screen. Scenery uses the calm groups (sky, meadow ladder, earth), never the touchable colours. Characters may wear their favourite colour: they stand on the ground with a shadow and the ink edge, while touchables float, bob and glow.
  • Shadows are purple-grey, never black or brown, in both looks.
  • Phase 1 has four world sets: the meadow (Pop Party and Peekaboo Garden), night, space and under the sea. The other kit worlds (evening, autumn, snow, beach) stay as they are and move onto this palette in Phase 2.
  • Dark worlds give touchables a soft glow so they stay the brightest things.
  • Ink is only for the thin edge and text, never for big areas.
  • New colours are added here first, then used, so the list stays the one source.
