Orlis UI kit

One paper look for the menus, the HUD, the phones and 3D games, drawn from the games themselves. Everything below uses only the tokens on this page. Shown at TV proportions, live.

Paper, not glassWarm paper backgrounds and cards with the white paper edge every cutout has. No blur, no grey glass.
One soft shadowOne shadow tone, straight down, the same purple-grey as the balloons' shading. Never black.
Fredoka speaksTitles, HUD, buttons and phones use Fredoka. Small print (settings, grown-up pages) uses Inter.
Painted icons onlyEvery icon comes from the asset library. No emoji, no system icons.
Big and few wordsAt TV distance: nothing under 2.6% of screen height; a picture and at most four words.
Colour means one thingA player's colour is always theirs; rose means "go"; gold means stars.
