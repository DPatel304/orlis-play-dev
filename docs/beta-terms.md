# Orlis Play beta: the short note (draft for the owner)

Shown once, before installing. Plain words, one screen.

**Thanks for testing Orlis Play!**

- **It's a test.** Things will break sometimes. If they do, tap "Send feedback" and tell us what happened.
- **Please keep it in the family.** Don't share the app or the invite link.
- **Your kids' things stay in your house.** Their names, avatars and progress are kept on your Orlis Box, not on our server. The camera
  watches for movement only; pictures never leave your Box.
- **Your grown-up account** (your email and sign-in) is on our server so you can sign in once and get updates.
- **You can stop any time.** Ask and we'll delete your account.
