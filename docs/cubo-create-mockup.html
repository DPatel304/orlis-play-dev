Cubo's Create

Make a game with your body, with Cubo as your guide. Every step is a little game with a few big things to touch, never a grid of small tiles. Cubo says each step once, in a few words, then stays quiet while you choose. A rough storyboard: the art is placeholder; the real screens use the painted worlds and Cubo's own animations.

Start

Cubo waves you in

The whole screen is the invitation. Your family's games sit along the bottom (3 slots).

"Let's make a game! Raise both arms to start."

Step 1 · Where

Step left or right to change the world

The world behind you changes as you move: meadow, beach, snow, space, night garden, and so on. Your outline shows where you are standing.

"Where shall we play? Step to look. Arms up to choose!"

Step 2 · The game

Pick what kind of game

Four giant choices: Collect, Dodge, Keep up, Stack. Each one shows Cubo doing it, so it reads without words. Touch one to choose. The job also decides how you play, so there's no separate "Your move" step.

"What kind of game? Touch one!"

Step 3 · What

Grab things off the turning wheel

First pop one of five big bubbles: Food, Animals, Sky, Toys or Nature. Then that group's things ride a slowly turning wheel, about 8 at a time. Reach into the glowing spot at the top as a thing passes to grab it. Swipe to spin faster, or pop a bubble to change group. Pick 1 to 5; they fill the tray. Arms up when you're done.

"What shall we collect? Grab it at the top!"

Step 4 · Rules (or skip)

Push the big sliders

Two giant sliders you push with a hand, with a much wider range: from a snail to a rocket (seven speeds), and from one thing at a time to a storm (about 25 on screen). Raise both arms to play, or just wait and Cubo picks for you.

"Fast or slow? Push it!"

Step 5 · Name

Jump to spin the name wheel

Goofy names, 3 to 5 words, made from your things and jobs. Jump to spin; arms up to keep it.

"Jump to spin! Arms up to keep it!"

Then

Play it straight away

The game starts in your world with your things. Afterwards: Play again, Change it (back into Cubo's Create at any step), or Home. It's saved in one of your 3 slots.
