Hide (10 seconds)

  • The TV goes dim and the seekers count out loud, the way real hide and seek starts. The numbers fill in as they go.
  • The hider runs in first person: a stick on the left to move, drag on the right to look.
  • Walk into a bush, a big flower pot or tall grass and you are hidden. No button to press.

Seek (60 seconds)

  • Each seeker's hand is a lantern on the garden. Hold it over a hiding place to peek.
  • The TV never shows the hider unless a lantern finds them. When the hider moves near a bush, it rustles: a fair hint.
  • On the phone the lantern light glows closer, the leaves show how hidden you are, and the sun sliding down is the clock (no numbers).

Found, or time's up

  • Found: the hider pops out on the TV as their own avatar (a paper puppet), waves and giggles. Time's up: the same, with "You stayed hidden!". Both are a win.
  • Then pass the phone: the next child hides. Rounds are short, about 1.5 minutes.
  • Gentle: no fail sounds, no scores to lose.

The garden

About 30 by 30 steps inside a hedge: a shed, a winding path, nine hiding places (three of each kind) and trees along the back. Dusk, fireflies, crickets.

A trial (0033)

Phase 1's bar is look and speed: the same world as Pop Party, 60 fps on the TV with tracking and on the phone. Scoring, more hiders and more gardens can come later.

One thing the render showed

Paper bushes look great from the TV's height but flat up close on the phone. Plan: hiding places are 3D shapes wearing the same painted skin, so both views match.
