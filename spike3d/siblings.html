What the 3D sibling keeps from the painted look
  • The painted sky, smiling sun and soft clouds, wrapped around the world so they turn with the camera.
  • Shade in the 2D shadow colour (a cool purple-grey), soft shadows under balloons, the horizon mist band, painted grass, flowers and petals as cutouts, and the paper grain.
  • The same palette, haze that softens far things, and faceted mountains and round bushes, as in the 2D art.
  • One flat shade tone on things and a soft white highlight on balloons, like the 2D balloons. A thin ink edge around balloons, bushes and characters, like the 2D pieces.
  • Balloons are the brightest things; scenery is calm and hazy, so they stand out the same way.
